## Shot data (measured by this film's author)

- **F1 received → strike 0.84 s**: Commons clip at 25 fps, frame of reception F035, strike frame F056, 21 frames.
- **F2 strike → in the net ≈0.9 s**: in the net at about F078; for a few frames before that the ball is smeared by motion blur against the advertising boards and the post and can't be seen, so it is estimated from the frames before and after, hence "≈".
- **F3 distance of the strike points** (`tools/pitch.json` → `tools/homography.py` → `assets/pitch.json`): one frame per goal (55′ highlights main camera F119, 61′ reverse high camera F156, 67′ Commons clip F056); in each picture 6–8 known pitch points are marked (corners of the penalty area and of the goal area, intersections of the penalty arc with the edge of the box; dimensions per the FIFA standard pitch: penalty area 16.5 m × 40.32 m, goal area 5.5 m × 18.32 m, penalty arc radius 9.15 m); a ground-plane perspective matrix is solved by least squares, residual ≤ 6 px; the point under the striker's feet is then mapped back to pitch coordinates: 55′ 5.1 m from the goal line, 7.7 m sideways (≈9.3 m); 61′ 7.7 m, 0.7 m (≈7.7 m, for the header the point under the take-off foot); 67′ 25.8 m, 6.4 m (≈26.6 m). The film rounds these to ≈9 / ≈8 / ≈27 m and notes " 1 " (error about 1 m).
- **12 minutes**: 55′ → 67′ (S1).
