# Facts on screen and in the shouts

*AI · Rhythm of AI, 1997 → 2026.* Every factual claim the film shows (level cards, year banners, name tags, props, price tags, remix labels, report card) or shouts, where it comes from and the source's own words. All strings are literals in `main_b.js` (search for the wording; the same strings are copied into the generated `main.js` and `index.html`). Shouts are in `voices/shouts.json`; subtitles `../game-show.srt` carry the shouts only. Times are film times from the tempo grid (`at(bar, beat)`, 150 BPM). The film was made in September 2026; checked on 2026-11-07.

Pages that refuse automated reading were read from the Internet Archive (the archived copy is linked beside the original), and posts on X through X's public embed endpoint.
