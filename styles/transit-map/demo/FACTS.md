## Figures checked and not used

- **First section: 6 km and 1993-05-28, not 4.4 km.** Chinese official and press sources give 6 km and 5 stations for —, opened for (sightseeing) trial operation on 1993-05-28 (tickets on sale from 1993-04-09 per ). The 4.4 km found in English Wikipedia is a different span, — (four stations). The film's title follows: .
- **"London took a hundred-odd years to reach 400 km"**: no reliable source gives the year London reached ~400 km. The time line states only what is sourced: London 1863 (6 km) → 2026 (402 km), 163 years; Shanghai 1993 (6 km) → 2010 (420 km), 17 years, → 2026 (906 km).
- **"The world's longest metro network"** is not claimed anywhere on screen: by the Ministry of Transport (Aug 2026) Beijing has 909.0 km and Shanghai 881.5 km (counted differently); Shanghai's own figure is 906 km.

## Counter rule

The route-length drums land only on sourced values (6.0, 16.1, 420.0, 906.0). Between 1995 and 2010, and between 2010 and 2026, the drums spin blurred and settle on the next value, so no unsourced intermediate figure is ever readable. During the first section (1.75–4.35 s) the counter measures the drawn line from 0 to 6.0.
